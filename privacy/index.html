MarketCall Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

This policy describes how Apes Labs, Inc. handles information in MarketCall after the transfer of the app and its service to Apes Labs. It takes effect when Apes Labs becomes the operator. For questions or requests, email contact@apes.app.

Information MarketCall handles

  • Account and profile: email address, sign-in details supplied through email, Apple, or Google, username, display name, bio, profile image, and any profile details you choose to add.
  • App activity: your market calls, confidence selections, scores, badges, friendships, groups, and related interactions.
  • Device and technical information: device identifiers or push tokens used for notifications, notification preferences, approximate location inferred from technical information where available, app usage, and diagnostics needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.
  • Messages to us: the information you include when you contact support or make a privacy request.

How information is used

We use this information to create and secure accounts, provide calls and scoring, show profiles and leaderboards, support friends and groups, send requested notifications, answer support requests, diagnose problems, and protect the service. MarketCall uses virtual points only; it does not process trading deposits, withdrawals, or cash prizes.

When information is shared

Some profile and activity information is visible to other MarketCall users through profiles, groups, and leaderboards. Service providers may process information for hosting and authentication, push delivery, diagnostics, and other app operations. These may include Supabase, Apple and Google sign-in services when used, and Expo or Apple notification services when notifications are enabled. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer.

Your choices and account deletion

You can update profile details in the app and control notifications through your device settings. To delete your account, open Profile → Settings → Delete account in MarketCall and confirm. You can also request deletion or ask about your information at contact@apes.app. Deleting an account removes associated data from the active service, subject to any information we must retain for legal, security, or backup purposes.

Security and retention

We use access controls and other safeguards appropriate to the service. We keep information while an account is active or as needed for the purposes described above, and then delete or de-identify it when it is no longer needed, subject to legal obligations and backup cycles. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as MarketCall changes. The date above shows the latest version. For support, privacy questions, or data requests, email contact@apes.app. You can also visit the MarketCall support page.
