Help center

MarketCall support

Need help with your account, a weekly call, points, groups, or a technical issue? Contact the MarketCall team at Apes Labs.

Email us

Send a description of the issue to contact@apes.app. Include your MarketCall username and app version if available. Never send your password or an authentication code.

Email MarketCall support

Delete your account

In the MarketCall app, open your profile, go to Settings, then tap Delete account and follow the confirmation prompts. This removes your account and associated MarketCall data from the active service. If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your account and ask for account deletion. We may need to verify the request.

About MarketCall points

Calls and scores use virtual points. MarketCall does not accept deposits or withdrawals and does not award cash prizes. Market information is provided for educational and entertainment purposes, not financial advice.

Privacy questions

Read the MarketCall Privacy Policy or email contact@apes.app with a privacy request.
